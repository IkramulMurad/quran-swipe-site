# Quran Swipe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Quran Swipe does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Bookmarks, reading position and settings are stored only on your device.
- Recitation audio is downloaded from EveryAyah.com the first time you play an ayah and is then cached on your device. Like any web request, this exposes your device's IP address to that server; the app sends no other information.
- Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
- Sharing an ayah uses the iOS share sheet. The app saves an image to Photos only when you choose "Save Image".

Contact: [open an issue](https://github.com/IkramulMurad/quran-swipe-site/issues/new) on GitHub, or see the [support page](index.html).
